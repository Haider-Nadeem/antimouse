# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 September 2026_

**antimouse** is a browser extension that provides a keyboard-driven search bar
for the current web page. This policy explains what it does and does not do with
your data.

## What antimouse accesses

To do its job, antimouse reads the content of the page you are currently viewing, specifically its links and buttons, so it can list them in the search bar.
This reading happens entirely on your device, in your browser, at the moment you
open the bar.

## What antimouse stores

The only thing antimouse saves is your **theme preference** (light or dark),
stored locally through the browser's `chrome.storage` API. Nothing else is
retained.

## What antimouse does NOT do

- It does **not** collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.
- It does **not** send the contents of the pages you visit to any server.
- It does **not** track your browsing history or activity.
- It does **not** use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
- It has **no** backend servers of its own; all processing happens locally.

When you choose a result that searches the web or opens a URL, your browser
navigates to that address just as if you had typed it yourself. antimouse does
not intermediate or log that navigation.

## Permissions

- **`storage`** — used solely to remember your theme preference.
- **Host access (`<all_urls>`)** — required so the search bar can work on any
  page you open it on. It is used only to read the current page's links and
  buttons, never to monitor your activity across sites.
